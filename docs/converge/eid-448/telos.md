## Operating Doctrine

Problems are recursively studied. A failed, blocked, stale, or surprising row
does not automatically become a coding task. It is classified first:

- `repair_item`: the cause is known and Docket can repair it.
- `research_loop`: the cause is unknown and Research must study it.
- `policy_gap`: the behavior requires a human authority boundary or approval
  rule from Governor.
- `praxis_drift`: the matrix, target, or proof envelope is stale or pointed at
  the wrong telos.
- `child_eidos_candidate`: the problem may need its own governance scope.

Child eidi are rare. A row becomes a child eidos only when it needs its own
telos, contracts, sustained steering, and evidence-graded research. Most rows
remain leaf loops inside this EID-448 eidos.
